Underwear Shopping Spree

Annapurna Kumar

Some of your underwear has holes. Protect your bone marrow! Protect your stem cells! Plug the holes! If you're too slow, you will become irradiated. If you're successful, win a shopping spree and compete for the high score.

Composed of soft sculpture and interactive projection, Underwear Shopping Spree is about fabrics that protect the wearer from extreme weather. Begun during the hottest summer on record in Southern California, the work was born from the delirious feeling of being dehydrated, irradiated, and overexposed.

Underwear Shopping Spree was installed at Human Resources Gallery, Los Angeles from January 1 - 10, 2025. The closing performance was cancelled due to the outbreak of the Palisades Fire and Eaton Fire.

About the Artist

Annapurna Kumar is an independent filmmaker who is textile curious. Based in Southern California, she makes animated films and soft sculpture, usually in response to the themes of extreme weather, technological skepticism, and liberation. Weaving together repurposed material culture with illustrations of daydreams and intuitive gestures, Kumar communicates her views in a playful, irreverent light.
