Sketches

Emily D'Achiardi

Sketches is part of an ongoing series of works that translates pixels into language. The project documents small moments of being such as my own personal drawings, screenshots, photographs, and videos and rewrites them into looping grids made of text. The original material never fully disappears, but gets filtered, translated, and remembered differently through the system. Each frame is broken into a grid, and areas above a certain brightness threshold are rewritten as repeated letters across the screen. What remains is less a clear representation of the landscape than a kind of textual residue of it.

About the Artist

Emily d'Achiardi is an artist from Atlanta, Georgia. She received a BA in Art History from Reed College and is currently an MFA candidate at the University of California, Santa Barbara.

She creates digital works that use the browser as a site of memory, intimacy, and emotional residue. Her work foregrounds fragments, repetitions, and the quiet systems that script online feelings.
