Kurdish Art

Ebrahim Alipoor

About the Artist

Ebrahim Alipoor is a Kurdish-Iranian photographer and visual storyteller from Baneh, Kurdistan. His work focuses on social issues, identity, and life in border regions, often exploring the intersection of politics, culture, and everyday resilience. He views photography as a powerful medium to engage audiences, particularly in regions where governmental restrictions and censorship pose significant challenges, and seeks to connect with people and inspire change through his work. Alipoor’s photographs have been exhibited internationally and published in major outlets, and he is a recipient of several international awards, including World Press Photo recognition. He continues to work on long-term documentary and art projects across the Middle East and Europe.
