ABOUT THE ARTIST

Amaia Gabantxo is a writer and a prolific translator of Basque literature, as well as a flamenco singer and, more recently, a soundscape artist and sound therapist. She studied literature and translation in Ireland and the UK and has taught literature, languages and creative writing at several universities in the UK and the USA. She now lives in the Basque Country, by the sea, where she continues to write, sing and weave sounds.
