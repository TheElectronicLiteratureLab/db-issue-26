Statelessness – An Artistic Perspective from Gaza

This feature explores the concept of statelessness as a partial response to the ongoing humanitarian crisis in Gaza, where many people live in a constant state of oppression, displacement, and uncertainty. Statelessness is not only a legal absence of nationality but also an existential experience that permeates every aspect of daily life, affecting both psychological well-being and the struggle to maintain one's identity.

As an artist, I have found that art becomes a language to express this experience, turning paintings, drawings, and video art into tools for documenting pain, memory, and, at times, imagining spaces of hope amid nothingness. Through my work, I seek to convey the feelings of those living without a homeland: the longing for a place that doesn't exist, the persistent attempts to find shelter, and the clinging to identity despite loss and absence.

About the Artist

Mohamed Harb is an artist from Gaza. He is a founding member of Artists' Shelter & Gaza Arts Gallery, an independent creative space dedicated to nurturing young artists and offering them a safe, supportive environment to develop their work in the heart of Gaza. The space blends contemporary artistic spirit with authentic Palestinian identity, providing a genuine platform for artists to express their human experiences amid the harsh realities of life in the Strip.
