Issue Credits

Editor-in-chief

Ravi Shankar

Pushcart prize winner Ravi Shankar is founding editor of Drunken Boat and a poet / translator / editor / memorist who has published 18 books including W.W. Norton's "Language for a New Century," called "a beautiful achievement for world literature" by Nobel laureate Nadine Gordimer. Translated into a dozen languages, he has published in The New York Times, Paris Review, Caravan, appeared on BBC, NPR and PBS, won fellowships from the Corporation of Yaddo, the MacDowell Colony and the Rhode Island State Council on the Arts and performed his work around the world. He teaches creative writing at Tufts University and for the New York Writers Workshop.

Managing Editor

Lili Newberry

Lili is a writer from San Jose, California. She is a student at Tufts University, where she studies English and philosophy, edits Currents Magazine, and sometimes makes jam. Lili has been awarded the Academy of American Poets College Poetry Prize for her poem “The Axe,” and the Morton N. Cohen Creative Writing Award for her chapbook, The Pink Highway. You can find her work in Midcult*, Whale Road Review, The Foundationalist, and more.

Nonfiction Editor

Daria Sommers

Daria Sommers is a writer and filmmaker who grew up in Bangkok, Thailand. A graduate of Oberlin College, she has been awarded grants from the New York State Council on the Arts and the National Endowment for the Humanities as well as Sundance and received fellowships from the MacDowell Colony and the Virginia Center for the Creative Arts. Her essays and reviews have appeared in Ginosko Literary Journal, Woman Around Town, and Art New England. Sommers is currently the Managing Editor of VBC Magazine and lives in New York with her husband. Her novel Sawadika American Girl (Vine Leaves Press, May 2026) is an intimate portrait of an American family living in Bangkok, Thailand at the height of the Vietnam War.

Fiction Editor

John Fried

John Fried’s short fiction has appeared in numerous journals, including The Gettysburg Review, North American Review, and Columbia: A Journal of Literature and Art. His debut novel, The Martin Chronicles, was published in 2019 by Grand Central Publishing. His magazine writing has appeared in various publications, including The New York Times Magazine, Rolling Stone, and New York, and Real Simple. He holds an MFA in fiction from Warren Wilson College’s Program for Writers and now teaches creative writing at Duquesne University in Pittsburgh.

Assistant Editor

Raga Chilakamarri

Raga Chilakamarri is a writer, journalist, and graphic designer from a small town in Massachusetts. She earned her B.A. in English and Economics from Tufts University in 2023, contributing features and arts pieces to campus publications and local papers like The Norfolk-Wrentham News and The Somerville Times. Awarded a Fulbright Grant to Spain, she lived in Madrid for two years, working as an English teaching assistant at the Camilo José Cela University and at a bilingual secondary school. While in Madrid, she also co-wrote and produced a Spanish/English short film called Provenance and created The Local Stranger Substack, for which she solicits, edits, and publishes essays inspired by place and travel.

Assistant Editor

Yagnya Misra

Yagnya Valkya Misra has dabbled in the fields of journalism and creative writing for over a decade and had his writing published in The Journal of Applied Communication Research, The Times of India, The Asian Writer, Yahoo, The Caravan magazine, Medium, The San Juan Daily Star, etc.

His short stories are anthologised in Happy Birthday To Me – A Collection of Contemporary Asian Writing (UK), Five Degrees: The Asian Writer Short Story Prize 2012 (UK), Celebrating India and Family Matters anthologies.

He’s been shortlisted for the 2011 Doris Gooderson Short Story Competition (UK), the 2012 International Student Short Story Competition (UK), The Asian Writer Short Story Prize (UK) in 2012 and also The Alpine Fellowship Writing Prize in 2023.

Yagnya is a recent MFA graduate in Fiction from Columbia University.
